Biography

My career has been shaped by environments where the stakes are real, the information is incomplete, and teams still need to move forward. From military service and construction management to strategy consulting, I have learned to value clear thinking, grounded leadership, and practical execution.

Today, I am exploring how those lessons apply to AI strategy and entrepreneurship. I am especially interested in helping leaders make sense of technological change without losing sight of the people responsible for bringing it to life.
